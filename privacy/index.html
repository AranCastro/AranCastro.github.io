Effective 26 September 2026

Privacy Policy

Your moods, tasks and circle stay on your phone. The app sends nothing over the internet; a message to your buddy is sent by you, from your own SMS or WhatsApp.

Who we are

Daybloom (“the app”) is developed by Dr Aran Castro. Contact: aran@draran.online.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your first name, as you typed it.
  • Which person in your circle is your buddy (or that the app should use the first person in Call anytime).
  • Your daily mood choices, one per day.
  • The tasks you add to the Eisenhower Matrix, with their quadrant, due date and completion status.
  • The people you add to your circle: name, phone number (optional), circle quadrant, and when you last reached them from the app.
  • Your reminder time and nudge settings, and a list of when a message to your buddy was offered, opened or declined.
  • Your best scores and play counts for the Play games.
  • The badges you have earned and when.
  • Your profile picture, if you choose one: a built-in avatar, or a photo you pick, copied into the app’s private storage. Photos are not included in backup files.
  • Any names you give to the matrix quadrants and circle sections.
  • Your garden: each flower grown, when, and what grew it (for example a task title or a game name).
  • Your focus-timer sessions (time, length, the flower grown and the linked task, if any) and any timer in progress.

If you add Daybloom widgets to your home screen, they show some of this information there (for example task titles, your streak, your garden or the names in your circle), where anyone holding your unlocked phone can see it. Widgets read the same on-device storage and send nothing anywhere. You can remove a widget at any time from your home screen.

This information is kept in the app’s private storage on your device. We do not run a server, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Erase all data deletes it.

Backups

You can make a backup of your Daybloom data from Settings → Backup and restore. The app writes one file (JSON) with the information listed above and opens your phone’s share menu; the file goes only where you choose to send it, for example your own Google Drive. The weekly backup keeps a copy in the app’s private storage on your phone and reminds you to save it. Restoring reads a backup file you pick and replaces the data on the phone after you confirm. Daybloom does not upload backups anywhere by itself.

If your phone’s own Google backup is switched on (Android settings), Android may include Daybloom’s data in that backup to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under their terms.

What leaves your phone

The app makes no network requests with your data. When your chosen number of low days in a row is reached, it shows a notification and a card on the Today screen. If you tap SMS or WhatsApp, your phone’s messaging app or WhatsApp opens with a short request to call you and your buddy’s number filled in; nothing is sent until you press Send there. The message never contains your moods, your tasks, your history, or the reason for it. Delivery is handled by your mobile operator or WhatsApp under their own terms.

Versions 1.7 and earlier sent this message through the public notification service ntfy.sh. From version 1.8 the app no longer contacts ntfy.sh.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or phone number collection.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking software.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No reading of your address book in the background, and no access to your location, camera, or microphone.
  • No upload of your circle’s names or numbers anywhere.

Permissions

Notifications: used only for your daily check-in reminder and the focus-timer alarm, both scheduled on your phone. You can turn it off in the app or in your phone settings.

Contacts: used only when you tap Choose from contacts. The app reads the name and phone number of each contact you pick and saves them on your phone. It never reads, copies or uploads the rest of your address book.

Photos: used only when you tap Upload a photo for your profile picture. Android’s photo picker shows your photos; the app receives only the one you choose and never uploads it. The app does not use the camera or microphone.

Calls and messages: Call, SMS and WhatsApp buttons open your phone’s own dialler, messaging app or WhatsApp with the number filled in. The app does not place calls or send messages itself.

For the buddy

Your buddy needs no app and no invitation. They receive only the message your friend chooses to send them by SMS or WhatsApp. We receive nothing about you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
