Nudge a Friend

Effective 25 September 2026

Privacy Policy

Your moods and tasks stay on your phone. The only thing that ever leaves it is one short line to the buddy you chose.

Who we are

Nudge a Friend (“the app”) is developed by Dr Aran Castro. Contact: aran@draran.online.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your first name, as you typed it.
  • Your buddy’s first name and a random topic code (for example nudge-85lixtexkrag).
  • Your daily mood choices, one per day.
  • The tasks you add to the Eisenhower Matrix, with their quadrant, due date and completion status.
  • Your reminder time and nudge settings, and a list of when nudges were sent.

This information is kept in the app’s private storage on your device. We do not run a server, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Erase all data deletes it.

What leaves your phone

When your chosen number of low days in a row is reached, or when you tap Send a test nudge, the app sends one message over HTTPS to ntfy.sh, a public notification service, addressed to your buddy’s topic code. The message contains your first name and a request to call you. It never contains your moods, your tasks, your history, or the reason for the nudge.

When the app opens, it also asks ntfy.sh whether your buddy has confirmed joining. That request contains only the topic code.

ntfy.sh is operated independently and keeps messages for a short period so they can be delivered. Its handling of messages is governed by its own terms, published at ntfy.sh.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or phone number collection.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking software.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Permissions

Notifications: used only to show your daily check-in reminder, which is scheduled on your phone. You can turn it off in the app or in your phone settings.

For the buddy

If you accepted an invite, the join page sends one message, “joined”, to ntfy.sh so your friend’s app can show that you are connected. We receive nothing about you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Health notice

Nudge a Friend is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, or emergency service. If you are in distress, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (India, free, 24 hours) or emergency services on 112.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
