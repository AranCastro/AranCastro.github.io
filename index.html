Daybloom

Grow a little every day.

Daybloom is one calm app for your mood, your tasks, your focus and your people, and everything you do grows the same garden. On the heavy days, it quietly lets help find you.

Nudge a Friend: help that arrives without asking

1

Tap once a day

Choose how today feels, from Bright to Heavy. No writing, no journaling.

2

Pick one buddy

A sibling, a close friend, a parent: someone who would want to know.

3

We nudge, quietly

After three low days in a row, your buddy receives one line: “Call Aran today.”

4

Nothing else leaves your phone

Your buddy never sees your moods and is never told why.

Sort your day with the Eisenhower Matrix

Place each task in one of four quadrants: Do first (urgent and important), Schedule (important, not urgent), Delegate (urgent, not important) and Later (neither). Today's screen shows what is due and what to do first. On a low day it shows only one task, because one thing is enough.

Know who to reach on a low day

Sort the people you trust into a second matrix: Call anytime, Quick call, Message first and Light chat. When today feels low, the app suggests one person to call and one to message, with one-tap Call, SMS and WhatsApp. It rotates through your circle so the same person is not always asked.

A small game that fits the day

Four short, calm games, with one picked for today's mood: Breathe on a heavy day, Bubble Pop on a low day, Pair Up on an okay day and Colour Clash when you feel good. No ads, and no timers you did not choose.

One garden for everything you do

Checking in, finishing a task, a focus session, a game, reaching out to someone and earning a badge each grow a flower in the same garden. Collect common blooms, rare finds such as Neelakurinji, and a Golden Lotus every twentieth bloom. The Pomodoro timer offers Gentle (15 min), Classic (25 min) and Deep (50 min) sessions.

Streaks that forgive

A daily check-in streak with a built-in rest day: after a week of check-ins, one missed day will not break it. Earn bronze, silver and gold badges for milestones, for coming back after time away, and for checking in honestly on a hard day.

Private by design

No account, no advertising, no tracking. Your moods, tasks and circle are stored only on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

If today feels too heavy

Tele-MANAS, run by the Government of India, offers free and confidential counselling at any hour. Call 14416. In an emergency, call 112.
